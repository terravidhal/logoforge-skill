# Trademarks

This repository contains instructions, example code, and screenshots. The example app
vendors unmodified logo component files downloaded from the Logoforge registry, and
`assets/readme/` contains screenshots that show those logos for illustration.

All product names, logos, and brands are the property of their respective owners.
A logo is used for identification only. Its presence does not imply affiliation with or
endorsement by the brand owner, and no brand shown in the examples is a customer, partner,
or integration of this project.

The MIT license covers this repository's text and code. It does not grant any right to a
trademark or logo file. Use a logo only to refer to the product it represents, and follow
the brand's own guidelines.

- Logoforge trademark and removal policy: https://logoforge.terravidhal.me/legal
- Removal requests: https://logoforge.terravidhal.me/legal#removal

If you are a brand owner and want a mark removed from the example screenshots, open an
issue on this repository or use the removal link above. Logoforge handles removal requests
within 72 hours.
